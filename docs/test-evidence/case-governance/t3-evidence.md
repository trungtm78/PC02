## HTTP contract for T4

Authoritative routes and DTO are `backend/src/cases/evidence-governance/evidence-governance.controller.ts` and service `EvidenceCommand`. All commands carry requestKey and expectedUpdatedAt; aggregate commands bind expectedRevision or expectedAggregateUpdatedAt where creation timestamps are the immutable version. Custody takes structured custodyFacts plus explicit expectedCustodyEventId (null on first receipt), not arbitrary operator JSON. Packet items accept assetVersionId and optional redaction/contentPolicy (PUBLIC_CONTENT_REVIEWED or REDACTED_DERIVATIVE); authority, policy hash, lineage and approval proof are server-owned.

Summary returns assets/custody/packets/holds/representationGrants/retentionPolicies/dispositions. Related assets retain caseId and add ownerCaseId/relationId/relationRevision. Packet summaries contain authorized exact items and contentsHidden when any owner/link is unavailable. Custody payload contains version/previousEventId/facts/currentCustody/sourceSnapshot. Verification returns success/data with assetVersionId,sha256,byteLength,integrityVerified=true,legalSignatureVerified=false. Export returns manifest/manifestHash/files (exact base64 bytes) and the offline-revocation notice.

Streaming boundary: authorization is rechecked before transmission. Revocation cannot recall bytes already sent or downloaded copies; offline verification establishes integrity against a separately trusted approved hash, not legal signature authenticity or current online revocation.
